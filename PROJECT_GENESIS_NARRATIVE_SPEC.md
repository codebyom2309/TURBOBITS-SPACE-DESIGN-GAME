# COSMO QUEST — PROJECT GENESIS: THE LAST SIGNAL
## Narrative, Campaign Progression & Cooperative Team Room Specification

### 1. Official Canon & World Premise
- **Title**: *PROJECT GENESIS: THE LAST SIGNAL*
- **Governing Organization**: **HORIZON COALITION** (*Interplanetary Survival Initiative*)
- **Starting Era**: Late Autumn 2026
- **Core Premise**:
  1. **Earth Under Pressure**: Accelerating megafires, agricultural drought stress, shifting ocean carbon cycles, severe tropical cyclones, and aging orbital observation & communications infrastructure leave humanity with critical blind spots.
  2. **The Anomaly (1.42 GHz)**: Deep-space listening stations detect a faint, structured **1.42 GHz hydrogen-line carrier signal** repeating every **72 minutes** from the direction of **Jupiter (Europa)**. Known as **The Last Signal**, it remains an unresolved scientific anomaly—never trivialized into a cliché alien invasion.
  3. **The Dual Mandate**: Space agencies and research institutions form the **Horizon Coalition** and launch **Project Genesis** with a dual mandate:
     - **Protect & Monitor Earth** with next-generation observation and orbital infrastructure.
     - **Build Interplanetary Capability** across Cislunar space, Near-Earth Asteroids, Mars, and the Outer Solar System so humanity can operate beyond a single planet and investigate the Jovian signal at its source.

### 3. Six-Stage, 25-Mission Connected Story & World Infrastructure Progression
Completing missions visibly upgrades six **Horizon Coalition Infrastructure Pillars**:
1. **Stage 01 — Earth Operations (3 Missions)**: *Wildfire Watch*, *Hurricane Sentinel*, *Global Ocean & Climate Mapper*.
   - **World Consequence**: Establishes the **Planetary Early-Warning & Biosphere Grid**, raising Earth observation coverage to 100% and calibrating LEO optical/thermal downlinks.
2. **Stage 02 — Orbital Operations (5 Missions)**: *Orbital Navigation & Relay Pathfinder*, *Orbital Debris & Magnetosphere Sentinel*, *ISS Supply and Crew Rotation*, *Design a Crew Shuttle*, *Orbital Outpost*.
   - **World Consequence**: Activates the **Orbital Logistics & Assembly Ring**, clearing LEO debris corridors, certifying reusable human transport, and commissioning an orbital construction dock.
3. **Stage 03 — Lunar Exploration (5 Missions)**: *Lunar Ice Prospector*, *Artemis Landing Scout*, *Lunar South Pole Relay & Topography*, *Lunar Polar Lander*, *Lunar Base Zero*.
   - **World Consequence**: Establishes **Shackleton Ice Extraction & Cislunar Relay**, unlocking lunar water-ice propellant staging and permanent human presence at Lunar Base Zero.
4. **Stage 04 — Asteroid & Planetary Defence (4 Missions)**: *Asteroid Prospector*, *Planetary Defense Kinetic Impactor*, *Asteroid Sample Return*, *Metallic Core Mapper*.
   - **World Consequence**: Commissions the **Deep-Space Sentinel & Sample Curation Vault**, proving kinetic asteroid deflection and autonomous microgravity rendezvous.
5. **Stage 05 — Mars Exploration (4 Missions)**: *Red Planet Climate & Storm Orbiter*, *Mars Subsurface Glacier & Relay Mapper*, *Mars Astrobiology Rover*, *First Humans on Mars*.
   - **World Consequence**: Builds the **Ares Surface Power & Habitat Precursor Network**, establishing humanity's first off-world research settlement on Mars.
6. **Stage 06 — Deep Space Exploration (4 Missions)**: *Venus Cloud Diver*, *Exoplanet Hunter*, *Europa Ocean Clipper*, *Outer System Plume & Ocean Flagship*.
   - **World Consequence**: Completes the **Interplanetary Deep-Space Array** and flies into Europa's radiation vault to sound the subsurface ocean and isolate the physical source of the 1.42 GHz Last Signal.

### 4. Cooperative Team Room Architecture
- **Team Identity**: Custom Team Name, Team Callsign, Mission Selection (any of the 25 missions), Collaboration Mode, and 6 Pixel-Art Team Insignias.
- **Four-Engineer Role Lobby**:
  1. **Mission Manager**: Mission planning, launch window, launch vehicle, partner agencies, budget & schedule oversight.
  2. **Systems Engineer**: Spacecraft bus, solar arrays, RTGs, batteries, attitude control, propulsion, and landing systems.
  3. **Science Lead**: Science objectives, payload instruments, and science phase duration.
  4. **Communications & Operations**: Transmitters, ground stations, flight computer, and radiation protection.
- **Real-Time Synchronization & Approvals**: Server-authoritative state (`/api/room/*`), live version polling, automatic approval reset on any design modification, role-enforced editing, and live Team Comms & Activity Log both in the lobby and on the engineering desk.
